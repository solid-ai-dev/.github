Open tools for building software with AI agents. Rules, checks and
templates that make your agents follow the same engineering standards
your team would.

## Projects

| Project | What it does | License |
|---------|--------------|---------|
| [solid-ai-templates](https://github.com/solid-ai-dev/solid-ai-templates) | Ready-made coding rules for AI agents. Compiles `AGENTS.md` and `CLAUDE.md` from modular templates for testing, security, Git, CI/CD and APIs, with pre-commit checks for the rules. | MIT |

## About the name

SAID stands for **S**olid · **AI** · **D**ev. The logo spells AID: a
pyramid for A, an obelisk for I and a half-sun on the horizon for D.

## Who is behind it

Built by [Branimir Georgiev](https://github.com/braboj) at
[Imbra](https://imbra.io). Imbra also offers an extended set of templates,
with stacks, platforms and process rules, on request.
